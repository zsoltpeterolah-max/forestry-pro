Why Forestry Pro Exists

A professional tool born from real field experience

The Problem We Solve

Walk into any forest management office in Europe, and you will find the same scene: printed volume tables from the 1960s, hand-drawn maps, and spreadsheets held together by hope. The forester heading out to the field carries a clipboard, a pencil, and decades of experience in their head.

They measure a tree. They write down numbers. Back at the office, they open a spreadsheet, look up a coefficient table, type in formulas, and hope they picked the right column. If the regulation changed last year, they might not even know.

The real competitor of Forestry Pro is not another app. It is paper, pencil, and a worn-out volume table.

We built Forestry Pro because foresters deserve a tool as precise and rigorous as the science they practice. Not a generic calculator. Not a research prototype. A field-ready instrument that speaks their regulatory language and produces audit-ready documentation.

What Makes This Different

Most forestry software is built for large enterprises, priced at hundreds or thousands of euros per year, and locked into desktop workflows. Small-to-medium forestry operations, independent consultants, and field technicians are left behind.

Forestry Pro fits in your pocket. It works offline. It costs a fraction of enterprise tools. And it covers more regulatory frameworks than most of them: 18 calculation methods across 12 countries, each verified against its official source document.
